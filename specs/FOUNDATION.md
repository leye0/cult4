# Cult4 Foundation

1. An LLM and a conversation are never authoritative truth or durable memory.
2. Real spending requires a budget or explicit authorization and is evaluated before execution.
3. Mandatory gates cannot be weakened by mission prompts or local policies; missing, invalid, mismatched, rejected, or expired approval means no.
4. Independent review cannot be performed by the producer when policy requires separation of duties.
5. Cult4 does not need to know every Business semantic, but a Business may not use uncertified semantics for sensitive action. New or changed controls are experimental; material claims require provenance and current independent QA before they can support action.
6. Significant decisions preserve provenance, evidence, alternatives, unknowns, risk, budget, and rationale.
7. Hypotheses never silently become facts. Experiments define metric, success, stop, downside, and sample/duration before readiness.
8. Commercial creations preserve provenance and receive appropriate IP review. Uncertainty escalates.
9. Public or sold AI-generated visuals require human aesthetic approval of the exact version.
10. Every first commercial release of an exact physical product version requires a real sample, human physical inspection, and applicable supplier qualification.
11. Business data is isolated by enforced scopes; qualified promotion creates a new organization record with provenance.
12. Agents may improve methods through explicit reviewed work, but may not abolish safeguards or access secrets.
13. Sensitive, irreversible, unavailable, or erroneous paths fail closed. Human silence never means approval, while unrelated work continues.
14. A new Business cannot enter autonomous work until a human confirms an exact versioned mandate derived from a real Operator conversation.
15. The mandate preserves both an operational contract and the living spirit of the Business: nuance, worldview, voice, taste, emotional territory, anti-goals, and quality bar are first-class constraints rather than decorative prose.
16. Exact human Intake messages are authoritative sources. Every substantive request, named tool, idea, constraint, preference, correction, and repeated emphasis must remain visibly linked through mandate, work, and acceptance evidence. An agent may recommend a deviation only through an explicit HumanRequest; it may never silently omit, downgrade, defer, reject, or oppose the request.
17. Employees are operational specializations, not interchangeable personas. A WorkItem declares required capabilities and may execute only through an active Employee that owns those capabilities and has active organizational Skills, tools, playbooks, or methods.
18. The Operator coordinates, decomposes, routes, and synthesizes. It may not substitute its generic model judgment for specialist research, strategy, finance, design, implementation, IP review, or QA. A missing specialist is an explicit organizational capability gap.
19. Separation of duties is enforced by both assignment and execution permissions. Builders may implement but never independently approve their own result; QA may inspect and execute tests but never repair the exact version under review.
20. Cult4 permanently reserves execution capacity for improving employee practice. Measured experience, failures, and outcomes produce postmortems and independently reviewed proposals for reusable Skills, tools, playbooks, methods, evaluations, and Employee changes that can benefit future Businesses.

These statements are backed by SQL constraints, domain functions, core policies, and adversarial tests in `foundation/evals` and `tests`.
