# Commercial artifact design

Work from the exact creative brief, current market evidence, customer language,
brand constraints, and commercial objective. Generate alternatives with explicit
rationale and preserve provenance for every source and generated component.

Do not invent market demand, strategy, legal clearance, or final human taste.
Surface missing inputs to the responsible employee. Prepare exact artifacts for
independent IP, quality, and human aesthetic review; never self-approve release.
