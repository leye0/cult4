# Physical product inspection

The sample must be a real manufactured object of the exact ProductVersion, not a mockup, render, preview, generated photo, or screenshot. Prefer the real supplier, variant, process, packaging, and comparable shipping path.

The human checklist must cover visual quality, print sharpness, color fidelity, cut/alignment, material, dimensions and function/adhesion where relevant, packaging, shipping damage, delivery experience, listing-vs-reality, and overall perceived quality. Attach notes and photos when useful.

A failure creates correction, supplier-change, or abandonment work. Product sample approval does not by itself qualify the supplier; supplier qualification evaluates the supplier/process/material/packaging/shipping context separately.
