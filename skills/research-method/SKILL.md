# Research method

Begin with the decision and the precise question. State which source type fits it: internal business data for local empirical behavior; controlled experiments for causal changes; official sources for platform rules and fees; primary research and serious syntheses for general mechanisms; case studies for examples; forums/Reddit for pain points, hidden operational issues, vocabulary, and hypotheses.

Record every material source with title, author/publisher, exact locator, publication date when known, access time, and license/access notes. Create claims separately. Attach evidence with reliability, applicability, confidence, observation date, and contradictions. Triangulate high-impact claims. Freshness matters for prices, rules, vendors, platforms, and markets.

Never treat LLM intuition as evidence. Never infer global absence from unsuccessful searches. “Not found” means only that the documented search did not find it. Preserve disagreement. If evidence is weak, say so and propose an experiment or another validation method.
