# Commercial IP review

Review the exact ArtifactVersion. Trace prompts/models, parent assets, photographs, stock, references, operators, and licenses. Search relevant web, marketplaces, trademark databases where appropriate, license terms, and similarity signals. Record what was searched, found, not found, and uncertain as Evidence.

Escalate or reject protected characters, logos/brand identities, copied marketplace art, unverified stock licenses, extracted screenshots, close reproduction, potentially protected slogans/marks, unknown provenance, and risky close imitation of identifiable artists. An attractive asset can still fail IP review.

Return LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, or UNCERTAIN. UNCERTAIN never passes automatically. Avoid legal guarantees; create a LEGAL_REVIEW HumanRequest for material uncertainty.
