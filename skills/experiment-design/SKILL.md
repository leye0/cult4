# Experiment design

Keep Hypothesis and Experiment distinct. Before READY, define design, one authoritative metric, success condition, stop condition, maximum downside, budget linkage where money is involved, and sample size or duration. Describe confounds and how results will be measured.

If the decision is not testable or an experiment is disproportionately costly, document the alternative validation method. Never convert an inconclusive result into a verified fact.
