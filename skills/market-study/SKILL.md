# Market study

Define the research question, target segment, market, language, and geography before searching. Choose validity according to the market's speed; shorten it when uncertainty or cultural velocity is high. A refresh must preserve the old study and preferentially revisit its communities, signals, uncertainties, and counter-evidence with new observations.

Research actual human behavior, language, preferences, complaints, fatigue, backlash, aesthetics, tropes, lifecycle, competition, and product fit. Separate `OBSERVED`, `ESTIMATED`, `INFERRED`, and `UNKNOWN`. Record sources, claims, evidence, contradictions, and metadata before synthesizing signals. Model memory is never current market evidence.

A complete study needs cultural, commercial, saturation, and opportunity conclusions. Commercial evidence may explicitly be `UNKNOWN`; cultural activity alone never proves willingness to pay. Search for counter-signals and document either contradictions or the search that found none. State methodology, limitations, uncertainty, confidence, and IP danger areas. Prefer a low-cost hypothesis and experiment when commercial evidence is unknown.

Never copy protected cultural expression. Surface the danger for independent IP review. Never make strategy for the Strategist or aesthetic approval for the human reviewer.
