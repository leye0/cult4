# Unit economics

Separate observed values, official fees, quotes, and assumptions. Calculate in integer minor units where persisted. Include price, discounts, taxes treatment, unit production, packaging, shipping, marketplace/payment fees, returns/refunds allowance, variable support/fulfillment, and acquisition cost where applicable.

Report contribution margin per unit/order/session as relevant, break-even, sensitivity ranges, cash timing, downside, and missing evidence. A favorable recommendation is not authorization to spend; use the Cult4 spend broker and its current budget.
