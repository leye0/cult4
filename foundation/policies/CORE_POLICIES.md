Culture-sensitive commercial subjects require an applicable, structurally complete, fresh MarketStudy and exact-subject approval by `CULTURAL_MARKET_INTELLIGENCE` before design commitment. The same evidence is rechecked for classified major investment and release. Market relevance never replaces creative, IP, physical, finance, supplier, or Git QA gates.

Businesses own their domain invariants and executable validation commands.
Sensitive external action requires a material Decision, provenance-backed
material Claims, and current independent QA validations produced by applicable
versioned Business controls. Changing a control version makes prior validation
ineligible. Existing unassured financial requests are invalidated rather than
grandfathered.

The executable registry is `src/policy.ts`. Local policies may only add restrictions.
